Problem Statement & Motivation

Why Autonomous Aerial Robotics?

Overcoming the limitations of manual reconnaissance and commercial consumer drones

Disaster Reconnaissance Hazards: First responders entering collapsed structures face extreme hazards (gas leaks, structural collapse).
The Golden Hour Imperative: Locating trapped victims within the first 60 minutes improves survival likelihood by over 78%.
Precision Agriculture Gaps: Satellite imagery lacks the temporal resolution and centimetre-level ground sampling distance (GSD) needed for early crop stress detection.
Consumer Drone Bottlenecks: Off-the-shelf drones require continuous pilot radio link, lack edge computer vision, and suffer from short battery endurance (<18 mins).

Control Theory & Math

6-DOF Non-Linear Mathematical Model

Newton-Euler translational and rotational equations of motion

Translational Dynamics (Earth Frame ENU):
m * d²r/dt² = R_B^E * [0, 0, T_total]' - [0, 0, m*g]' - F_drag
Rotational Dynamics (Body Frame):
I * dω/dt = τ_body - ω × (I * ω)
Inertia Tensor: Ixx = 0.0285, Iyy = 0.0285, Izz = 0.0510 kg·m².
Actuator Dynamics: First-order motor response model with 40ms time constant.

Avionics & Automation

Cascaded Dual-Loop PID Controller

Attitude rate, angle, altitude, and trajectory tracking loops

Inner Rate Loop (400 Hz): Controls angular rates (p, q, r) directly producing motor torque commands.
Middle Attitude Loop (100 Hz): Converts roll/pitch/yaw angle errors into target angular velocity demands.
Outer Position Loop (50 Hz): Converts 3D waypoint displacement errors into target vehicle tilt angles.
Anti-Windup Integrator: Clamped back-calculation preventing integrator saturation during step transients.

Autonomous Navigation

3D A* Path Planning & Mission Generation

Collision avoidance, survey grids, and SAR search patterns

3D A* Heuristic Search: Evaluates Euclidean distance to generate collision-free trajectories around obstacles.
Search & Rescue Spiral: Archimedean expanding spiral trajectory maximizing ground sensor coverage in disaster areas.
Precision Agriculture Grid: Boustrophedon (lawnmower) path with 75% lateral overlap for orthomosaic mapping.
Failsafe Autonomous RTL: Safe altitude climb to 15m, translation to home, and smooth vertical descent.
